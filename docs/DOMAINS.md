# Business domain ownership and reclaim

## First claim
1. Sign in using a verified company mailbox whose registrable domain matches the website.
2. Open **Verified score badge**, enter the root website, and generate the verification file.
3. Publish that exact file on the exact origin and verify it.
4. A successful proof creates the exclusive organization claim. A pending challenge does not reserve a domain.
5. The administrator can subsequently connect supported child origins and release the organization's claim through an explicit confirmation.

Matching uses the public suffix list, including private suffixes. Personal mail providers do not qualify. First organization proof must be on the registrable root or its www origin; a delegated subdomain cannot claim the entire organization.

## An administrator has left
Open **Verified score badge → Has your company administrator left?**

Provide your company website, full name, company, role and reason. Sonar requires a verified matching business account and prepares a draft addressed to:

- To: support@pactuno.online
- Subject: Reclaim domain - {domain name}
- From: your matching company email
- Details: request reference, role, reason and proposed proof of current company authority/domain control.

Click **Open email draft**, choose the matching company sender in your mail client, and send it yourself. Preparing a draft sends no email and changes no ownership. Personal or unrelated-domain email requests are not accepted. Repeated preparation reuses the pending reference.

Support manually verifies mailbox authenticity, company authority and fresh domain control before any transfer. The email subject or matching From address alone is not sufficient proof. Never send passwords, API keys, database exports or customer data.
